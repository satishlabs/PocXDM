Queue rows are cleaned only after successful send. Failure reverts to PENDING; destType=`2` remains.

1. Observe or force bundled send `isSuccess=false`.
2. Confirm no `STEP-12A` for that batch; rows still in queue.

## Expected

No `STEP-12A`  
Queue destType=`2` still present
